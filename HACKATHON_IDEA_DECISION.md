## Required gates before another selection

These are user-driven strategy gates, not additional event rules:

1. **A documented problem:** identify a specific person, recurring situation, present workaround, and meaningful cost or frustration. Seek independent evidence and separate anecdotes from population estimates.
2. **An unmet need:** compare the closest products on the same user, inputs, decision, constraints, and completed action. State exactly where their documented workflows fail the target situation. Absence from marketing pages alone is not proof of missing functionality.
3. **Substantive differentiation:** explain in one sentence what changes the outcome. A new name, audience label, interface, or ordinary feature bundle is insufficient without evidence of value.
4. **Resistance to obvious convergence:** check sponsor demos, past entries, and generic model suggestions. Treat overlap as a warning and independent generation as a heuristic, never as proof of global uniqueness or knowledge of private entrants.
5. **Essential Qloo contribution:** identify a plausible advantage over an equally informed LLM and simple baseline, then require measured evidence before claiming it. A different ranking alone does not establish usefulness.
6. **Clear presentation and feasibility:** only candidates that clear the preceding gates enter the event-rubric tournament and first-use storyboard.

There is no validated replacement from this audit. The other eliminated ideas do not inherit the crown. Future discovery must begin with documented unmet decisions rather than a list of possible taste-API applications. No search can guarantee that another entrant will not independently propose a similar idea.

## Essential Qloo mechanism

1. Accept explicit films/music the user likes; resolve ambiguous names and let the user confirm identities.
2. Apply factual constraints to a fixed, named book shelf. Examples: already read, language, a verified page-count ceiling. Do not infer a diagnosis or personality.
3. Use Qloo to rank the remaining book entities against the cultural inputs. The LLM controls the workflow and clarifies ambiguity; Qloo contributes the cross-domain ordering.
4. Show one choice with a short factual explanation. If available, display Qloo's input-attribution data separately from editorial wording. It describes model contribution, not personal enjoyment probability.
5. On rejection, distinguish a factual constraint from taste. Exclude an already-read book; ask what “not for me” means rather than inventing negative affinity. Re-query and choose a replacement.
6. Open an official publisher sample, a verified lawful reading page, or a clearly labeled catalog record. No fictional local availability or reproduced copyrighted excerpt.

[Insights documentation](https://github.com/qloo/docs-public/blob/main/reference/insights-api-deep-dive.md) supports explicit result sets and input attribution. The [current harness starter](https://github.com/qloo/qloo-hackathon-kit/blob/main/starter/mcp-client/README.md) supports ranking and clarification states. **The actual event credential must still prove these work together.** Generic documentation is not an entitlement test.

Remove Qloo while keeping the same LLM, metadata, shelf, interface, constraints and explanation budget. If the result is equally useful, the hackathon premise fails. Raw Qloo affinity cannot grade its own superiority; [scores are contextual and query-normalized](https://github.com/qloo/docs-public/blob/main/docs/interpreting-affinity-scores.md).

## Honest first-use story

- **0–3 seconds:** “You know what you love watching. Find the book you want to start.” Three editable taste chips and one clearly labeled sample case; no account.
- **3–10 seconds:** One action returns one book, a brief explanation and “Start a sample.” Any cached example is labeled and never presented as a fresh query.
- **Next interaction:** “Already read” or an explicit length preference changes the choice. The user can see which constraint removed the old suggestion.
- **Credibility beat:** Expand “How this was chosen” to inspect matched entities, supported attribution or its absence, factual metadata and an optional same-shelf baseline. The normal flow stays simple.
- **Completion:** A working source link, not a saved list or a chat promising future help.

This is a storyboard, not a fabricated demonstration. No example recommendation, measured latency or success percentage exists yet. If legitimate samples are not consistently reachable, the promise must shrink and the ranking be reconsidered.

Official publisher samples exist—for example [Macmillan's The Terraformers page](https://academic.macmillan.com/academictrade/9781250228062/theterraformers/)—but the eventual shelf needs its own link/rights review. [Open Library availability](https://openlibrary.org/dev/docs/api/search) does not prove local-library stock. Do not make the user supply private Shopify or Capital IQ data.

At least 8/12 users must favor Qloo across their tasks (within-user ties count against this gate). Publish all denominators and failures. |
| 32–40h | Repeat the relevant comparisons with shuffled cultural seeds; separately check understanding and action completion with 10 fresh users. | Real seeds beat shuffled seeds in ≥16/24 comparisons; at least 8/10 fresh users explain the value and reach a valid sample unaided. | 40–48h | Review results against these written gates; optionally evaluate a minimal SideStreet challenger if authorized and resources allow. | Pass, narrow, switch, or reopen according to the rules below. No automatic second build. |

This small convenience sample is an exploratory go/no-go signal, not a population estimate or statistically established efficacy. Repeated tasks are clustered within participants, not 24 independent people. Preserve user-level results. If recruitment is unavailable, mark utility **untested**; do not substitute the models' aesthetic votes or synthetic profiles.

Baseline design: both methods see the same confirmed inputs, complete candidate pool and lawful factual metadata. The LLM baseline receives no Qloo ranks or attribution. Freeze model/version/prompt and use equal explanation length. Judge selections before explanations to detect persuasive-copy effects. The simple baseline combines a predeclared media-to-book genre mapping with within-shelf popularity; record the popularity source and handle missing values consistently. If this source is unavailable, report that limitation rather than silently changing the baseline after results.

The first-use test is distinct from the choice test. A person clicking a publisher sample establishes flow completion, not that they read or enjoyed the book. No habit, literacy or health claim follows from this sprint.

## Crown-transfer and kill rules

- **Pass ShelfBridge:** access/rights, coverage, factual correctness, blinded utility, signal control and first-use gates all pass. Proceed only within later granted implementation scope.
- **Downgrade:** attribution missing, one metadata field unreliable, or the shelf must be named more narrowly. Remove the unsupported claim and rerun affected tests. A useful but Qloo-neutral product does not pass the sponsor gate.
- **Pivot:** if choices are good but the external sample route causes failures, repair the route or make the result a clearly labeled catalog choice; reassess whether that weaker ending still warrants selection.
- **Kill:** no advantage over either baseline, shuffled seeds equally useful, systematic entity failure, inability to provide lawful actions, or unresolved hard-constraint errors. Do not polish past the failure.
- **Transfer to SideStreet only if it independently passes:** two frozen destination pools of 30 places each; ≥90% identity/location mapping; the same blinded Qloo-vs-LLM/popularity and real-vs-shuffled thresholds with appropriate consenting users; zero invented operating facts; ≥8/10 unaided completion; workable quota/latency. A two-city boundary is acceptable if explicit. Geography-only self-reconstruction and colorful rank movement cannot pass it.
- **If both pass:** ShelfBridge remains preferred unless the challenger shows a clearly larger preference advantage (at least four additional wins out of 24 against each baseline) while matching first-use completion and practical reliability. Treat this as a decision heuristic, not statistical significance across different tasks.
- **If neither passes or the study cannot run:** reopen selection or leave the recommendation conditional. Do not automatically crown Encore or SponsorFit.
