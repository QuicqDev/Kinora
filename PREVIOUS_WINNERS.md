Inspected 2026-10-02. **These are 2025 Qloo LLM Hackathon precedents, not winners or rules of the current Agentic Hackathon.** Four winners is the verified complete set; six non-winning comparison entries bring the corpus to ten. Quality beats padding the winner count with unrelated awards.

## Strongest adjacent competitors

The subsequent [eight-direction originality audit](ORIGINALITY_AUDIT.md) extends this comparison with NoveList/Libby, HopWatch, Pass Culture, AbleVu, festival-clash tools, Showtime, Basil and Edelweiss. It also records Qloo's own retail-assortment positioning. No newly identified winner or award is claimed. CratePlan's possible distinction is confined to an event-specific constrained stock-selection workflow; it is not the invention of recommendations, assortment planning or packing manifests.

- **Group choice:** [CultureCircle](https://devpost.com/software/culturecircle) already claims a group Harmony Score and multi-domain recommendations. A new group app needs a distinct negotiation/proof mechanism; rebranding a score is insufficient.
- **Reading:** [PersonaReads](https://devpost.com/software/personareads) uses Qloo for book discovery. [NoveList Select](https://about.ebsco.com/resources/novelist-select-overview) integrates recommendations in catalogs, while [NoveList's library guide](https://about.ebsco.com/sites/g/files/nabnos191/files/acquiadam-assets/NoveList-Plus-Scavenger-Hunt-for-Public-Libraries.pdf) already discusses movies/TV as discovery inputs. A finite shelf and cross-domain entry point must earn utility, not claim category invention.
- **Booking:** [Soundcharts](https://help.soundcharts.com/en/articles/3192479-what-s-compare-artists) already supports comparisons for bookers/programmers; [its API](https://developers.soundcharts.com/api/reference/artist/get-similar-artists) exposes related artists. [Advise](https://advise.events/how-it-works) covers artist comparisons, and [Prism](https://prism.fm/) covers booking operations. A replacement act agent cannot rely on “similar artists” as novelty.
- **Festival attendance:** [Clash](https://www.getclashapp.com/) and [Frontstage](https://app.frontstagefestivals.com/) already cover discovery, schedules and coordination. This eliminated the attendee-itinerary variant.
- **Sponsor's own demo:** [Qloo](https://www.qloo.com/capabilities) demonstrates cross-domain cultural/place exploration. The entry needs a useful completed task beyond the API's showcase.

## What transfers, and what does not

### Follow-up originality audit — 2026-10-02

Closer current product evidence supersedes the earlier willingness to select ShelfBridge:

- [Bookfinity](https://bookfinity.com/reader-type-quiz/start): the recommendation quiz explicitly includes movie and television tastes.
- [Bookshelf by Huda's Apps](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.huda.bookshelf): its developer listing explicitly offers book discovery from favorite movies. The title contains “Google Books”; this does not make it a Google-developed app.
- [Embertale](https://embertale.app/): advertises reading-slump help, selection from an existing shelf, constraints and preference feedback. Its creator's efficacy claims are not independently validated here.
- [Mapstr Matching](https://en.mapstr.com/fonctionnalites/mapstr-matching-artificial-intelligence): saved favorite places inform recommendations in other cities, directly overlapping SideStreet's hook.

These pages establish advertised competing propositions, not exhaustive feature equivalence. Their existence does not prove our ideas were copied; it does invalidate treating the central hooks as distinctive without a demonstrated unmet need. Both recommendations have been withdrawn; see [current decision](HACKATHON_IDEA_DECISION.md).

### Historical strategy observations

Our strategic inference: specific workflows, a visible transformation, and memorable wording are better selection signals than agent counts. Preserve the same inputs/candidates in before/after comparisons; make limitations visible; finish the last useful step. The artifacts do **not** establish that these features caused the awards.

Do not copy GeoTaste's business-location concept, Alloy's corporate-fit claims, Zesty's inversion, or Resonance's targeting. The older event's demo requirements, dates and API anecdotes are not current rules. Current submitted competitors remain unknown because the [2026 gallery](https://qloo.devpost.com/project-gallery) is unpublished.
