## CratePlan — “Two crates. Which books go?”

**Proposition:** help a pop-up bookseller select an event-specific assortment from books already owned, within carrying capacity, then repair it when stock or capacity changes.

### Evidence of the decision

- [A pop-up seller's account](https://www.reddit.com/r/booksellers/comments/1t4cmxe/inventory_woes/) describes limited stall space, inventory cost pressure and tracking genres by market. This is an anecdote, not a prevalence estimate or proof that existing-stock selection is the principal problem.
- [Hugo House's bookseller-led session](https://hugohouse.org/event/industry-talks-here-for-a-good-time-not-a-long-time-running-a-pop-up-bookstore_2026-06-12/) emphasizes focused collections, conservative buying and the short period to recover investment. It does not test AI.
- [The Heath Bookshop](https://www.theheathbookshop.co.uk/pop-up-bookshop/) sells at music festivals; [Paper Trail](https://onthepapertrail.com/pop-up-events) already tailors stock to event themes. These establish the workflow and human-curation baseline.
- [Susan Ravdin's Book Fairs 101](https://www.defreitasbooks.com/writings/bookfair101.html) describes both repacking for each event and repeating a stock selection. It is an experienced dealer's account, not a controlled result; its displayed update date is malformed.

### Exact competitor boundaries

| Alternative | Already does | Remaining proposed distinction, unverified |
|---|---|---|
| [Basil](https://basilsoftware.com/new-bookstores) | Fair shipments, returned stock, sales reconciliation, offsite operations. | Selection of which already-owned titles to take based on confirmed cultural event anchors. Packing lists themselves are not new. |
| [Edelweiss Omnibus](https://www.edelweissplus.com/omnibus) and [NAB² pilot](https://www.nab2.org/nab2omnibuspilotprogram) | Inventory and offsite/mobile/pop-up operations. | Cannot claim the customer segment is ignored. |
| [Edelweiss Analytics](https://www.edelweissplus.com/edelweiss-analytics) | Own/peer sales and inventory analysis, stock recommendations and action lists. | Event with no prior sales history, constrained to existing stock; superiority over peer-sales advice remains unknown. |
| [Qloo itself](https://www.qloo.com/) | Markets cultural assortment and merchandising. | Broad cultural assortment planning is not our invention. Only a specific operational workflow could be differentiated. |
| Experienced bookseller, theme rules, informed LLM | Event-aware selection and feasible lists. | These may already solve the problem adequately; they are serious baselines. |

**Novelty judgment:** low-to-moderate confidence in a distinctive workflow, low confidence in a defensible product gap. We found no exact full match in the inspected materials; we did find nearly every component and the broad sponsor use case. Do not describe this as globally unique, a new algorithm, or proven demand.

### Mechanism and honest scope

An operator confirms a small number of film/music anchors that describe the event, along with a fixed stock list, capacity in copies, and must-carry titles. These anchors are contextual hypotheses, not verified attendee preferences. Qloo ranks the same candidate pool for each anchor; the application uses ordinal ranks or top-k membership to propose an assortment under explicit constraints. It never sums normalized affinity across people or reports audience coverage percentages. The LLM resolves ambiguous names, calls tools, asks about missing information and repairs the plan after changes.

Capacity in copies is an explicit simplification. Do not claim a physical crate fits a mix of editions without measured dimensions/weights. Quantities remain owner-controlled; Qloo affinity is not a sales forecast. The distinctive claim must be a better assortment decision, not the solver alone. Display useful off-theme discoveries only when supported, without treating novelty as usefulness.

**First ten seconds, proposed:** a clearly labeled sample inventory of 100 titles becomes a selection of 30 copies; the remainder stays visible. Reducing capacity repairs the selection and preserves locked books, or explains an impossible constraint. These counts are storyboard examples, not measured results. A fresh user can inspect and edit the stock, constraints and event anchors without a private retail integration.

**Data route:** use a bounded set of [Wikidata structured facts under CC0](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing), original descriptive text and visibly synthetic stock/capacity for a public sample. Keep factual source links and edition identity. Open Library's [licensing page](https://openlibrary.org/developers/licensing) does not give blanket CC0 permission for all contributed material or covers. Actual dimensions and stock need owner input. No cover art or copied catalog prose is assumed licensed. Qloo caching/output terms and entity coverage remain untested.

**Fatal assumption:** event-linked cultural affinity makes stock selection meaningfully better than an experienced seller, genre/theme matching or an equally informed LLM. This is not established. If all value comes from the capacity solver, the hackathon premise fails.

## Repertory Slot — “One open Thursday. Which restoration?”

**Proposition:** help a small cinema starting a strand without useful audience history shortlist titles from an operator-confirmed bookable pool, using cultural anchors and locality affinity.

The problem is documented by [ICO's programming course](https://www.independentcinemaoffice.org.uk/course/practical-programming/). The [2026 Art House Convergence survey](https://arthouseconvergence.org/ahc-news/2026-national-audience-survey-press-release) shows enthusiasm for repertory among respondents recruited through participating cinemas; it is not a representative survey of everyone in those cities and does not demonstrate failure of present programming.

Qloo could provide cultural/locality ordering; the agent enforces runtime, already-screened exclusions and verified booking constraints. Proposed first use: one empty slot and a confirmed pool become three options with an inquiry link. A distributor listing is not a booking guarantee. A locality-conditioned rank versus one without locality is a model comparison, not “local demand versus national demand.” Exact geography support for the chosen output category must be checked before implementation.

[Showtime's audience-intent workflow](https://www.showtimeanalytics.com/news/ukca-conference-2024) is a strong direct alternative. [Europa Cinemas](https://europa-cinemas-blog.org/2026/06/22/bologna-day-3/) also reports rights-discovery services and existing AI scheduling. No inspected source proves an underserved cold-start segment where Qloo beats those alternatives.

**Fatal assumption:** aggregate locality affinity helps a real programmer choose a more useful title despite rights, marketing and schedule constraints. Best next test would involve an authentic confirmed slate and programmers' independent choices. Different cities' annual-report hits cannot validate this: available films, schedules, capacity and selective reporting confound the comparison. Park this candidate; no authenticated data, rights confirmation or evaluation is available.

## Pre-registered next validation plan — not executed

The next uncertainty is observable user need and sponsor value, not another model's ranking. The following exploratory thresholds are decisions for a later authorized study, not guarantees or population estimates. No sellers, programmers, permissions or usable credential are assumed available.

| Stage | Method | Go / downgrade / stop |
|---|---|---|
| Problem, before prototype | Six consenting experienced pop-up sellers describe recent unfamiliar-event stock decisions before seeing the concept. Ask for incidents, time spent, rejected choices, current tools and consequences. | At least 4/6 describe a consequential subset-selection problem; at least three can provide a permitted anonymized plan/stock artifact. Otherwise stop or redefine the problem. General cash-flow complaints do not pass. |
| Novelty/workflow | Compare those actual tasks against Basil, Edelweiss, seller methods and a competent generic LLM workflow. | If an existing workflow solves it acceptably, stop. Product-page omission is insufficient. |
| Access/data, hours 0–4 after separate authorization | Verify event credential, supported ranking route, output terms and correct identities on a frozen 100-title pool. | At least 90% correct mapping and no silently guessed identities. Check all selected titles and hard facts. Unmapped titles remain explicit manual choices; do not hide them to inflate results. |
| Sponsor isolation, hours 4–16 | Same stock, metadata, constraints, solver, UI and explanation budget. Compare Qloo ranks, equally informed LLM ranks, event-theme/popularity mix, owner plan and shuffled event anchors. | Off-theme counts/rank movement are diagnostic only. Different outputs do not pass utility. |
| Exploratory utility, hours 16–36 if evaluators available | Eight consenting experienced sellers, two tasks each; blinded/counterbalanced comparisons, ties count against the candidate. Log useful edits and final approval time. | Qloo preferred in at least 11/16 task comparisons against each automated baseline, with at least 6/8 seller-level positive balances against each. For seller-level balance, two tasks give win=+1, tie=0, loss=-1; balance must be positive. Against the unaided plan, at least 6/8 judge quality no worse and median approval time falls at least 25%. These are small-sample decision thresholds, not statistical proof. |
| Correctness and first use, hours 36–48 | Ten frozen stock/capacity/locked-title cases, including infeasible inputs; ten fresh representative users if available. | Zero invalid stock/capacity outputs; all infeasible cases explained; at least 8/10 complete unaided. Correctness alone cannot offset failed usefulness. |

**Pass:** only after problem, gap, data, sponsor utility and operational gates all pass would CratePlan become a provisional build candidate. **Downgrade:** narrow catalog, explicit capacity units or manual identity handling while preserving utility. **Pivot:** if operator control/packing helps but Qloo adds nothing, retain that as a possible ordinary tool and reject it for this event. **Kill:** no meaningful unmet problem, existing solution adequate, no baseline advantage, or signal-shuffled choices equally useful. Missing participants means untested, not passed.

Neither interviews nor this small comparison establishes increased sales. That claim would require a later field study accounting for event attendance, prices, display and stock exposure. Historical sales logs alone are also selected by what the seller chose to bring.

## Final research judgment

The broad problem and operator workflow are real. A unique, valuable gap is not yet established. This audit therefore produces a defensible research lead and an explicit rejection record, not another overconfident winner. No evidence justifies calling any current candidate unique or claiming it will solve the problem better. The next discriminating evidence is from actual workflow incidents and fair comparisons; more speculative consensus would repeat the original error.
