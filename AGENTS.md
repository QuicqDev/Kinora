For ideation, read RESEARCH_HANDOFF.md and use claude-opus-research, hackathon-idea-forge, and codex-claude-collab. Give Claude the Idea Forge methodology and governing principles. Research previous winners, verify each other's claims, critique constructively, and record evidence, disagreements, decisions, Claude model identity and persistent sessions.

Keep event-specific rules separate from historical examples and strategy assumptions. Research and planning are authorized; implementation and external publishing require a subsequent user request. Preserve research in this project, and ignore local Claude session storage in Git.
